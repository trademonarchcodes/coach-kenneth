05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
Start here.

Here are a few basics to help you understand what affiliate marketing involves.

What is affiliate marketing?+

Affiliate marketing is a way to promote another company's product or service using a unique tracking link. A commission may be paid when a qualifying sale happens through that link.

Do I need to create my own product?+

No. Affiliate marketers promote products or services offered by other businesses, subject to each programme's terms.

Can I start with my phone?+

Many parts of affiliate marketing, such as learning, creating content, and sharing links, can be done with a smartphone and an internet connection.

Will I earn money immediately?+

There is no guaranteed income or timeline. Results depend on many factors, including the product, audience, content, effort, and programme rules. Some people may make no commissions.
